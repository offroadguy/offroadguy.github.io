APPLE · GPU PLATFORM & MODEL SERVING

Getting models from weights to production

I owned the GPU Kubernetes cluster lifecycle, capacity planning, scaling, and production incident response. I built API-backed onboarding tooling that staged weights from S3 to FSx, verified checksums, and integrated with Argo CD and Flux.

I ran pre-onboarding inference evaluations with a Principal Development Engineer. We varied input and output length, concurrency, batching, precision, KV-cache settings, and GPU parallelism, then measured latency, throughput, memory, errors, cost, and output quality.

APPLE · DISTRIBUTED SEARCH & RETRIEVAL

Making vector search faster at fleet scale

I owned Solr 9.9 infrastructure, provisioning, infrastructure as code, and performance across roughly 200 nodes in multiple regions. I also co-owned Elasticsearch and Milvus infrastructure with the team.

I led the migration of roughly 200 Solr nodes to Graviton, using validation tooling to preserve query throughput. The migration reduced that infrastructure cost by 19.8%, or about $266K annualized.

FREDDIE MAC · HIGH AVAILABILITY & DISASTER RECOVERY

Modernizing a platform that had to stay recoverable

Through Cloudwick, I helped replace manual VMware workflows with cloud-native infrastructure and automated deployments. I led mortgage-document migration work from Hadoop and Solr to Elasticsearch/OpenSearch and S3, and built recovery automation using Airflow and Python.

The modernization included active-active US East and West infrastructure. The delivered hybrid-cloud recovery environment achieved an approximately 15-minute RPO and 2-hour RTO.
